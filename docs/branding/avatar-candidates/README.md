# Provisional GitHub avatar candidates

These files are internal review material. They do not select an avatar or
change a GitHub repository, organisation, Pages, favicon, release, tag, or
alias.

## Candidate A — KlumAST repository avatar (recommended)

The repository candidate is a text-free square crop of the approved Season 4
documentation artwork. The crop retains the product-specific model, luminous
chamfered portal, diagonal catwalk, and foreground shadow. Its 720-by-720
source-artwork crop starts at `(250, 20)`, giving the portal frame equal
90-pixel space from the upper and left crop edges while revealing more of the
catwalk toward the lower right. The candidate export stylesheet hides only the
four non-icon lockup paths; it does not redraw the artwork. This makes the
shared geometry and the KlumAST-specific figure readable without relying on
lettering that would fail at avatar scale.

Accessible description: “A stylised model steps from a luminous chamfered
portal onto a diagonal ivory catwalk, with a soft shadow extending toward the
foreground on a deep navy ground.”

Visual source: `docs/user/img/season-4/klumast-season-4-documentation.svg`.
The controlled editable source for that published artwork remains
`docs/branding/season-4/editable/klumast-season-4-documentation-text-master.svg`.
`provisional-klumast-season-4-repository-approved-artwork-source.png` is a
lossless 1680-by-945 `rsvg-convert` rendering of that approved output, and
`exports/provisional-klumast-season-4-repository-avatar-crop-source.png` is
the retained 720-by-720 crop source. Together with the controlled editable
master, they make the export reproducible without redrawing the embedded
approved model or introducing a competing mark.

## Candidate B — KlumDSL suite/organisation study (not recommended for selection yet)

`provisional-klumdsl-suite-avatar-study-source.svg` is a clearly separate
proof of the visual-language document’s open-portal/two-unroped-stanchions
idea. It is an editable, text-free square `(250, 20, 720, 720)` crop derived
from the controlled Season 4 header master. Like Candidate A, its portal frame
has 90 pixels of source-space at the upper and left edges and the same
proportional height and placement. Its final wordmark path and the original
wide-header lower-edge dissolve are omitted; that dissolve would otherwise
read as a horizontal line across the square catwalk. All remaining portal,
catwalk, and stanchion geometry stays intact.

Accessible description: “An open luminous chamfered portal with two unroped
stanchions opens onto a diagonal ivory catwalk on a deep navy ground.”

This study is useful if the maintainer wants a suite-facing symbol that does
not borrow KlumAST’s model. It should **not** become the KlumDSL organisation
avatar without a maintainer identity decision: the visual language explicitly
lists it as a future candidate, and the partially closed-door alternative is
also unselected. Reusing Candidate A for the organisation would make a strong
KlumAST mark do double duty, but it would blur repository and suite identity.

## Exports and reproducibility

The reviewable outputs in `exports/` are generated by
`export-provisional-avatar-candidates.sh`; no generative artwork is used.
Regenerate them from the repository root with:

```text
sh docs/branding/avatar-candidates/export-provisional-avatar-candidates.sh
```

GitHub can be given the retained `1024` PNG for either selected avatar. GitHub
derives the smaller display sizes from that upload, so no generated `512`
through `16` PNGs are versioned. The earlier small-size visual inspection is
recorded below and can be reproduced with the export script by changing its
single size value locally.

## Repository social-preview candidate

`exports/provisional-klumast-season-4-github-social-preview-1280x640.png` is
a separate, provisional 2:1 social-preview candidate for the KlumAST
repository. It is not an avatar and does not change the repository setting.
The crop starts at `(250, 69)` in the 1680-by-945 approved documentation
artwork and measures 1310-by-655 before its proportional 1280-by-640 export.
At this scale, the portal frame starts just over 40 pixels from the upper edge,
has roughly 88 pixels at the left edge, and the complete lockup retains more
than 40 pixels at the right edge. Those are the only GitHub-safe details. The
navy background, light cone, catwalk, and figure shadow intentionally continue
into the surrounding area and may meet the card edge.

Its source is the same controlled documentation master described for Candidate
A, rendered without the avatar-only stylesheet as
`provisional-klumast-season-4-social-preview-approved-artwork-source.png`.
The crop source and final PNG are retained in `exports/` and are regenerated by
the same export script. The final PNG is designed for the GitHub social-preview
field but remains pending maintainer selection and upload.

## Visual review

Before the derived review sizes were removed, the `1024`, `256`, `64`, and
`32` PNGs were visually inspected. At `64` and `32`, the repository candidate
kept the portal, model silhouette, and diagonal catwalk readable; fine figure
detail correctly receded. The suite study kept the portal, two stanchions, and
catwalk readable, and therefore proves a distinct suite direction rather than
a second KlumAST crop. Both were also inspected at `64` on white and GitHub's
dark-surface colour `#0d1117`: their deep-navy square edge remains clear on
light surfaces, while the ivory portal/catwalk preserves recognition on dark
surfaces. Neither candidate relies on text at avatar scale.

## Favicon boundary

Candidate A has no text and is structurally suitable to evaluate as a future
KlumAST docs-favicon source. It is not a favicon implementation: its crop is
new review material, it does not replace `docs/user/img/klumlogo.png`, and no
change to issue #724 is made or authorised here. A #724 owner may reuse it
only after the KlumAST candidate itself is selected and that issue's own
favicon requirements confirm the square crop and small-size rendering.
